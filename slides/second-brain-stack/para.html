About 1 minute. PARA is Tiago Forte's method and the most common default. Four folders, sorted by how soon you'll act: Projects have a goal and a deadline, Areas are ongoing with no end date, Resources are topics you care about, and Archives hold anything you're done with. Things move right over time: a finished project goes to Archives. Our brain already has the P (projects/), and notes/ works like Resources. To switch, tell your agent: "Reorganize my brain into PARA and update the map in README.md."
