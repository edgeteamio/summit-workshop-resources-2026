Not connected yet? It tells you exactly what to add.

Tested Oct 29 on the Mac app: Muse saved the instructions to its memory, checked the repo, and put a GitHub connector card right in the chat. The connector went live in late September and Meta listed it with the iPad launch on Oct 7. Connect opens GitHub sign-in in your default browser. Before it was connected, I asked it to save a decision, and it gave the exact file name and text to add, as the short instructions say. Reading and writing through the connector still needs a check on a real login. Muse can also build a custom connector from a token, but Meta doesn't review custom connectors, so only give it a brain-only token. Meta Muse runs on iOS, Android, the web, WhatsApp and Mac.
