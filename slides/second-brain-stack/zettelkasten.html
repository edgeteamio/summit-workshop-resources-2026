About 1 minute. Zettelkasten means "slip box" in German. The sociologist Niklas Luhmann kept about 90,000 index cards, each holding one idea, with an ID like 1a1 so a new card could sit next to the card it grew from, and he linked related cards by ID. It suits writers and researchers. The cards on the slide are this workshop's own ideas, linked the way Luhmann would. In markdown, a link is just [[note-name]] or a normal link, and agents can follow links as well as you can. It's more work to keep up than PARA, so most people who try it end up with a mix.
