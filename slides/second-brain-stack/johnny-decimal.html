Work → Workshops → Summit 2026. Say the number, find the thing.

Up to 10 areas, 10 categories in each, and never more than two folders deep.

About 1 minute. Johnny.Decimal (johnnydecimal.com) gives everything an address. Up to ten areas, each a range like 20–29. Up to ten categories in each area, like 22. Then numbered items inside a category, like 22.01. The folders never go more than two levels deep, so nothing gets lost in a deep tree, and you can say "it's in 22.02" out loud. It suits people who mostly want to find things fast: admin, finance, client files. For an agent, the numbers make paths short and predictable, and the map in README.md can list the areas.
